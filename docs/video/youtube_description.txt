FieldPilot: Nemotron plans a spray-drone operator's day (Physical AI)

FieldPilot by Future Agri plans a spray-drone operator's whole day: which farm first, which battery set flies it, whether the weather lets the spray land, and how many litres each refill needs.

- NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Lightning on Nebius Token Factory reads WhatsApp bookings in Hindi, Hinglish and English as schema-constrained JSON; code checks every number against the message.
- The day planner schedules every farm against the live forecast, India's 2023 drone SOP, and the operator's battery sets and charger.
- At the farm, Nemotron 3 Super runs the field agent's tools and Nemotron 3.5 Super VL reads the zone photos behind a calibrated conformal gate. A WOFOST crop twin and Tavily advisories decide what is worth spraying, and safety checks plus a human approve every flight.
- For the closed JIYI K3-A Pro controller, the pilot gets a flight card. On open firmware, the same plan flies over MAVLink.

What is real and what is simulated:
- Drone footage: our T1 10 L quad, FieldPilot drone runs.
- Desk and field console: FieldPilot.exe running live on our laptop with Nemotron on Nebius Token Factory (demo bookings, example zone photos, drone simulator).
- Labelled SIMULATED: the 5-country benchmark, the K3-A Pro twin, the simulated field day and ArduPilot SITL.

Music: original, synthesised for this video. Voice: synthetic (Kokoro).
Code and every result file: https://github.com/Aryan221224/fieldpilot

Sources for the numbers on screen: DJI Agriculture annual report 2025 (about 600,000 agricultural drones in use); Feminism in India ground report, 4 Jun 2026 (8–9 min of spraying per battery, about 2 h to charge); Dainik Bhaskar via Navbharat Live, 15 Sep 2025 (90 % of Drone Didis in Madhya Pradesh at a loss).
