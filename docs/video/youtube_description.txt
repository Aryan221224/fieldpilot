FieldPilot: Nemotron plans a spray-drone operator's day (Physical AI)

FieldPilot by Future Agri plans a spray-drone operator's whole day: which farm first, which battery set flies it, whether the weather lets the spray land, and how many litres each refill needs.

- NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Lightning on Nebius Token Factory reads WhatsApp bookings in any local language (the demo uses Hindi, Hinglish and English) as schema-constrained JSON, so the same tool works for operators anywhere in the world; code checks every number against the message.
- The day planner schedules every farm against the live forecast, India's 2023 drone SOP, and the operator's battery sets and charger.
- At the farm, Nemotron 3 Super runs the field agent's tools and Nemotron 3.5 Super VL reads the zone photos behind a calibrated conformal gate. A WOFOST crop twin and Tavily advisories decide what is worth spraying, and safety checks plus a human approve every flight.
- For the closed JIYI K3-A Pro controller, the pilot gets a flight card. On open firmware, the same plan flies over MAVLink.

Global impact (ESTIMATE from our test benchmarks, counting only 25 % of the measured gain):
Agricultural drones sprayed about 210 million hectare-treatments in 2025 in China and the USA alone. In our test benchmarks FieldPilot used 76 % fewer battery cycles, 77 % less flight time and 98 % less wasted tank mix per useful hectare. A quarter of that, applied to today's drone work, saves per year: about 35 M battery cycles, USD 65 M of battery wear, 73 M litres of wasted mix, 49 GWh of charging, 3.4 M pilot flight hours and 22 kt of CO2. Method, sources and tests: eval/global_impact.py, tests/test_global_impact.py, eval/results/global_impact_2026-10-30.md (10 % and 50 % cases included).

- Simulated flights: the 5-country test benchmarks behind the global estimate, the K3-A Pro twin, the simulated field day and ArduPilot SITL.
